# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients evaluating Ruth Oropeza for frontend or full-stack engineering work.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors quickly understand Ruth's engineering capabilities and contact her about relevant opportunities.

## Positioning

The portfolio emphasizes turning slow or unclear digital experiences into reliable, conversion-focused platforms.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the portfolio to assess technical capability, review selected projects and experience, and decide whether to start a conversation. The site should support Spanish and English versions to reach a broader audience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present skills, projects, experience, and contact information in a clear single-page portfolio.
- Present available certifications with direct PDF viewing.
- Preserve the existing project and experience facts.
- Support Spanish and English content where the product provides both versions.
- Do not invent links, metrics, testimonials, or other proof.
- Keep the site usable on the web across desktop and mobile layouts.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the name Ruth Oropeza.
- Preserve the portfolio's professional engineering focus.
- Support Spanish and English versions for greater reachability.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing portfolio implementation in `src/`.
- Project data in `src/data/projects`.
- Existing experience, skills, and testimonial content in `src/components/sections/`.
- Existing image and brand assets in `src/assets/` and `public/`.
- Any links, metrics, testimonials, and supplied images are the source of truth; future work must not fabricate replacements.

## Product Principles

- Make engineering capability understandable quickly.
- Lead with concrete project evidence rather than unsupported claims.
- Connect technical work to outcomes visitors can evaluate.
- Preserve factual content while improving clarity and reach.
- Make contacting Ruth a clear, low-friction next step.
